## What the text establishes

The supplied terms expressly permit collecting database content through the API
for analysis and presenting processed results with proper attribution. They
state that downloaded Content is covered by Creative Commons Attribution 4.0,
including copying, distribution, transmission and adaptation without specific
permission from MP when proper attribution is given.

[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) requires appropriate
credit, the licence link, retention of supplied notices and an indication of
modifications; do not imply endorsement or impose incompatible restrictions.
It permits commercial use. This data licence is separate from Eka's MIT licence.
The terms also prohibit direct website scraping and request advance contact for
large downloads. This project uses the API; this review makes no new download
and sends no message to MP.

## Application to this project

Use the supplied terms as the working basis for preparing attributed research
results and covered MP data. A separate provider permission request is not a
blanket prerequisite for uses already permitted by this grant. Do not label the
grant absent simply because our web reader could not retrieve the page.

The snapshot's retained MP fields are `material_id`, `composition`,
`formula_pretty`, `theoretical`, `database_IDs`, and `deprecated`. Derived
artifacts include canonical composition groups, provenance labels, splits,
rankings, aggregate metrics and plots. The general Content grant supports their
preparation with appropriate attribution insofar as they are covered content;
this is not independent field-by-field provider confirmation.

Preserve MP citation, database version, retrieval date, source route and changes
(normalization, grouping, labels, splitting and scoring). Keep GNoME excluded.
MP identifiers do not grant rights to separately obtain or redistribute the
underlying ICSD/Pauling/other source records. No such original records or crystal
structures were requested by the exporter. Retain any specific exception or
source notice encountered during final artifact review.

## Evidence limits and release status

The owner-supplied copy does not include its browser URL or a licence-version
date, but the independent read-only page check above confirms that its material
licensing provisions match the official page visible on the review date. The
page does not display an effective date for the Content grant. Its 10 October
2011 effective date belongs to the privacy-policy section and must not be
represented as the date of the CC BY 4.0 grant. This remains a project evidence
review, not an institution's legal opinion.

The source-only Git archive has a separate [release-readiness review](release-readiness.md).
Review any proposed data bundle for exceptions, credentials, provenance and
attribution before publication; do not automatically add datasets to Git. Seek
clarification only for a concrete unresolved exception or repackaging question.
No data release or external message is made by this review.
